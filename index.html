01Why converge

Built for the people who run the room

Not a marketing conference. A working one — for organisers, registration leads and the teams who own attendee experience.

01

Attendee management

Hands-on tracks on intake forms, validation, de-duplication and clean attendee records that survive the day of the event.

02

Three-day agenda

Eighty-six sessions across six tracks, with studio hours where you bring your own registration stack and rebuild it.

03

Open data standards

Every attendee record on this site is stored and exported as portable JSON — no proprietary lock-in, no export fees.

04

Hospitality included

All passes include breakfast, lunch, the Thursday reception and access to the quiet-room and childcare programme.
